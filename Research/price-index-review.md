# Price index: items to review

0 items where the sources disagree too much to be sure. Each is priced from the market: the PvP flea, PvE flea and trader prices (the median of them when there are three, otherwise their average, with the handbook counting for a fifth of a market price so it can only nudge). **SPT's price isn't used**; it's shown for comparison only. "First pick" is what the normal rules chose before that. For items marked (ContentBackport), the SPT and Handbook columns are WTT ContentBackport's own prices. To set a price by hand, add it to `Research/tools/price-index-overrides.json` and run `price_index.py` again.
